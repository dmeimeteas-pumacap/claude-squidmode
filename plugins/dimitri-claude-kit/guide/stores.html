▤ Where things live

Everything the kit reads or writes, all of it under ~/.claude. Two columns matter: what owns a file, and whether it is safe to edit by hand. Editing a derived file is harmless but pointless — it gets regenerated. Editing a canonical one is fine.

Reading this page Three words do all the work.

Canonical

  • The real thing. If it disagrees with anything else, it wins. Safe to edit.

Derived

  • Generated from something canonical. Edit it and your change disappears on the next regeneration.

On first use

  • Does not exist until the relevant command runs. Its absence is not a problem.

If you back up one thing

Back up threads/, goals/, memory/, and CLAUDE.md.

Why those four

They are the only genuinely irreplaceable parts. The kit itself reinstalls in a minute; your months of decisions and reasoning do not.

What to leave out

janitor/, plugins/, .kit-backups/ — all regenerated or reinstalled. And be deliberate about projects/: session transcripts can contain anything you ever pasted into a conversation.
