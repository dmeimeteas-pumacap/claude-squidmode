Start here Four commands cover almost every day. The rest can wait.

The loop, in order

  • 1/today — decide the handful of things you're doing.
  • 2/log — whenever you stop or figure something out.
  • 3/catchup — when you come back and can't remember where you were.
  • 4/eod — at the end, to see what the day amounted to.

The three pairs people mix up

  • /goals vs /today — standing areas vs this one day.
  • /reconcile vs /deepclean — wrong records vs stale files.
  • /grill-me vs /scrutinize — while deciding vs after deciding.

Worth knowing up front

  • Claude never invokes /eod or /eow itself. You type them — or put /eod on a schedule, which the kit ships setup for.
  • /change-review is always on. There is nothing to run.
  • Prefer it interactive? Ask Claude /kit and name what you're after — a command, a topic, or just what you're trying to do.
  • /help is Claude Code's own help, untouched. This kit does not take it over.
